SMMC Race Results

User Manual – Capture Results on Race Day

What this page does

The Race Results page lets you record who finished where in each race, works out the standings for you, and produces a results file to hand over for processing. It works seamlessly on a phone, tablet, or PC.

You only need to do five things:

  1. Pick the regatta
  2. Tick who is racing
  3. Capture each race
  4. Check the results
  5. Send for processing
Tip: A phone or tablet at the water's edge works best. Hold it upright (portrait mode).

Practice Mode (try it first!)

Practice mode lets you try the whole process with sample sailors. Nothing is saved to the club system and nothing is sent, so you can't break anything.

  1. On the regatta list, tap Practice mode.
  2. An orange bar appears at the top saying PRACTICE. Tap the Practice regatta.
  3. Follow sections 1 to 6 below as normal. Select the sailors, add races, tap the boats, check the results.
  4. At the end, tap Send. It will tell you nothing was sent. This is correct.
  5. Tap Exit practice in the orange bar to go back to the real regatta list.
Tips
  • Want a fresh start? Tap Reset in the orange bar to clear your practice races.
  • Try the tricky bits: hold a boat for DNS/DNF, delete a race, drag boats to reorder.
  • The list includes one guest sailor so you can see how guests appear.
  • Suggestion: Do one practice run at home before your first real race day.
Need this manual again? Tap the ? icon at the top of the page, or open the menu and choose Help / User Manual.

1. Select the Regatta

When the page opens, you will see a list of upcoming races, with the soonest first.

  1. Look for your race by date, class emblem, and name (e.g. DF95 Handicap).
  2. Tap the race to open it.
Tips:
  • Tap a class emblem at the top to show only that class. Tap All to see everything again.
  • Use the search box – typing a class, round, or date (e.g. DF95 or 03/10) shortens the list.
  • Races from the last 3 days are still shown, so you can finish yesterday's capture.
  • A small tag on the right shows your progress: Entries set, In progress, or Processing (already sent).

2. Select the Competitors

The page shows every active member of that class, plus any registered guests (shown with their home club).

  1. Tap a name to tick it. Tick everyone who is racing today.
  2. To tick everyone at once, tap Select all. Tap again to clear.
  3. Check the counter at the bottom (e.g. 9 competitors selected).
  4. Tap Confirm entries.
Tips:
  • Suggestion: Tap Select all, then un-tick the people who didn't turn up. It's much quicker!
  • You need at least 2 competitors before Confirm entries turns on.
  • Names are sorted A–Z. The #number is the sail number.
  • Entries can be changed any time until the first race is captured – tap the entries bar at the top of the race screen.

3. Capture a Race

Starting a race

Tap Add Race 1 (the button is at the top right of the Races screen).

You will see two columns:

  • Left: Finish order (Empty slots numbered 1, 2, 3…)
  • Right: Boats still to place (One tile per boat, showing its sail number)

Recording the finishing order

  1. As each boat finishes, tap its sail number tile.
  2. It automatically jumps into the next free slot on the left (1st, then 2nd, and so on).
  3. Continue until every boat is placed.
Tips:
  • Suggestion: Have a helper call out sail numbers while you tap – it helps keep up with fast finishes.
  • From Race 2 onward, each tile shows the boat's overall rank (e.g. #3 o/a) and tiles are ordered by current standings, helping you find a boat quickly.
  • Tapped the wrong boat? Simply tap its filled slot on the left and it goes back to the right.

Boats that didn't finish normally

Press and hold (about half a second) on a boat's tile. A menu will appear:

Code Meaning
DNS Did not start
DNF Did not finish
RO Race Officer this race (not scored for this race)

Tap the code you want. The boat moves to the left under the finish order.

Tips:
  • Don't tap-and-release – you must hold. A quick tap places the boat as a standard finisher.
  • To undo a DNS/DNF/RO, tap its orange or green row once.
  • Suggestion: Place the DNS/DNF/RO boats first, then the finishers. It makes it obvious when you're done.

Finishing the race

  1. When all boats are placed, the header will say "All boats placed – ready".
  2. Tap the glowing + button (top right). The race is saved and Race 2 starts straight away.
Tip: Changed your mind mid-race? Tap the X (top right) to cancel the race you are capturing. Nothing is lost from earlier races.

4. Fixing and Reviewing Races

From the Races list, tap any captured race to open it.

  • Reorder boats: press and drag the ⋮⋮ handle on the left of a row up or down.
  • Change a result: press and hold a row, then choose DNS, DNF or Finished.
  • Move between races: use the left/right arrows at the top.
  • Insert a missed race: on the Races list tap the + Insert race line between two races. Race numbers update automatically.
  • Delete a race: tap the bin icon next to it and confirm. Later races renumber automatically.
Tips: Entries lock once Race 1 exists. If you must change entries, delete all races first (only do this if nothing has been sent). Deleting cannot be undone.

5. Check the Results

Tap the menu icon (top right, three lines). You can open:

  • Regatta Entries – who is entered
  • Scoresheet – raw finishing order for each race
  • Race Results – the calculated standings
  • Send for Race Processing – see section 6
  • Change regatta – go back to the race list
  • Exit – close the page

To end racing, tap Finish on the Races screen and confirm. The final standings table opens.

How the scoring works

  • Position = points (1st = 1 point, 2nd = 2 points…). Lowest total wins.
  • DNS / DNF score the number of entries plus 1.
  • RO boats are left out of that race completely.
  • Drops (discards): 1 after 4 races, 2 after 8 races, then +1 every 8 races. Dropped scores appear struck through.
  • Ties are settled by countback (most better places, then most recent race).

6. Send for Race Processing

  1. Open Race Results and tap Save Results (top right), or choose Send for Race Processing from the menu.
  2. (Optional) Type or paste the race report into the box and tap Save report text.
  3. Tap Send for Race Processing.
  4. A file named like 370EF1BB-27-06-2026.json is downloaded to your device.
  5. Hand that file to whoever processes results. The page then returns to the race list and marks the regatta as Processing.
Tips: Check the results table carefully before sending. Fix any mistakes first. The race report is completely optional.

Good to Know

  • Auto-save: Everything is saved on your device after each step. If you close the page or lose signal, reopen it, pick the same regatta and carry on.
  • Same device only: Saved work lives in that device's browser. Capture and send from the same device and browser, and don't clear your browsing history mid-regatta.
  • Internet needed: Internet is needed to load races and members. The capture itself keeps working once loaded.

Troubleshooting

Problem What to try
Race list is empty Check your internet, then refresh the page. Check the class filter is on All and the search box is blank.
Member list is empty Go back (menu → Change regatta), and open the regatta again.
A sailor is missing Guests must be registered beforehand. Ask the club admin to add them.
Wrong boat placed Tap its slot on the left to remove it, then place the correct boat.
Tap placed a boat but I wanted DNF Tap the slot to remove, then hold the tile and choose DNF.
Can't edit entries Entries lock after Race 1. Delete all races to unlock (before sending only).
Page looks stuck Refresh. Your captured races are saved and will reappear.

Quick Race-Day Checklist

  1. Open page → tap your regatta
  2. Select all → un-tick no-shows → Confirm entries
  3. Add Race → tap boats as they finish → hold for DNS/DNF/RO → +
  4. Repeat for each race
  5. Finish → check results
  6. Send for Race Processing → hand over the downloaded file
